I'm **Özge Çinko**.
My name means *unique* and *special*, and my surname means *zinc*.
So yes, I guess I come with built-in chemistry. 👩🏼‍💻

I'm currently an AI Engineer at ING, working around agentic AI. Before ING, I spent two years as an **AI Research Engineer at Huawei**, where I worked on research-driven AI systems, including **recommender systems**. I hold a Bachelor’s degree in **Computer Engineering** from Sakarya University.

For me, engineering is a creative craft: a way of turning curiosity, emotion, and ideas into experiences. I care about building technology that feels more purposeful, more human, and more alive.

I love researching, building, learning, speaking, writing, and exploring ideas through art, technology, and meaningful conversations.

In every idea I share, every word I write, and every project I build, I try to leave a piece of who I am.
